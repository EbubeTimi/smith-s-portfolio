NorthQuest · Creator Program Operations Platform

30+ person teamContinuously iterated
Visit the live platform ↗

The operations platform behind the fintech's UGC program, built and then refined through round after round of iteration until the daily routine runs itself. Creators log posts through a tracker that enforces a 2-posts/day cap by server timestamp and catches duplicate video links. A 12-step onboarding flow takes each new creator from welcome screen to signed contract. The payment register calculates every creator's pay live from logged posts, and the 99-profile database keeps private fields sealed away from the public app. The compensation model was migrated from per-video rates to flat monthly salaries with no service interruption.

CashDrive · Creator Payroll & Sales-Attribution System

Live in productionSingle-session build

A separate system for the automotive program, built around what makes car sales different: a vehicle-sale and bonus tracker that attributes each confirmed sale to the creator whose content brought the buyer in. Discretionary bonuses stack into an auto-calculated payments register with no manual re-entry. Creator intake collects payment details and a typed contract signature in one four-step flow, and a WhatsApp help desk routes account and contract questions with pre-filled messages.
