NorthQuest — Creator Program Operations Platform

30+ person team8-week build

A full operations platform for the fintech's UGC program: a 12-step onboarding flow with contract e-signature, a daily posting tracker that enforces a 2-posts/day cap by server timestamp and catches duplicate video links, an automated payment register calculated live from logged posts, and a data-integrity audit that finds hidden-Unicode name splits and orphaned records across the 99-profile database. Payment reminders go out through a one-tap WhatsApp flow. Also directed a live compensation-model migration from per-video rates to flat monthly salaries with no service interruption.

CashDrive — Creator Payroll & Sales-Attribution System

Live in productionSingle-session build

A separate system for the automotive program, built around what makes car sales different: a vehicle-sale and bonus tracker that attributes each confirmed sale to the creator whose content brought the buyer in, with discretionary bonuses that stack into an auto-calculated payments register — no manual re-entry. Creator intake collects payment details and a typed contract signature in one four-step flow, and a WhatsApp help desk routes account and contract questions with pre-filled messages.
